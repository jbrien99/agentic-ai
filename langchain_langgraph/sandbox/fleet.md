# Tesla Model Y vs. Ford Mustang Mach-E for a 100-Car Sales Fleet

| Category | Tesla Model Y | Ford Mustang Mach-E |
|---|---|---|
| Starting price | About $41k–$42k | About $39,995 |
| EPA range | Roughly 280–321 miles depending on trim | Up to 320 miles depending on trim |
| Charging | Strong advantage from Tesla Supercharger network and trip planning | Up to 150 kW DC fast charging; broad public charging support |
| Cargo / seating | 5 seats; about 75 cu ft max cargo space | 5 seats; about 59.7 cu ft max cargo space |
| Service / maintenance | Low EV maintenance, but Tesla service/body repair access can vary by region | Low EV maintenance and wider Ford dealer/service network |
| Fleet risk | Strong charging ecosystem, but regional service variability | More conventional dealer support, but earlier recall/uptime history matters |

## Recommendation

**Choose the Tesla Model Y** if charging reliability and maximum fleet efficiency are the top priorities. For a 100-car sales fleet, its Supercharger access, slightly stronger cargo utility, and long-range performance make it the better all-around fit.

**Choose the Mustang Mach-E** only if your organization values Ford dealer familiarity and easier local service coordination more than Tesla’s charging ecosystem.

**Bottom line:** the **Tesla Model Y** is the stronger fleet choice overall.
